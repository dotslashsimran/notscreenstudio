# notscreenstudio v0.1.0

The first downloadable build of notscreenstudio, a free screen recorder for macOS in the style of Screen Studio. Everything stays on your Mac. There are no accounts, no network calls and no telemetry.

## What it does

- Records a whole display, a single window, or an area you drag out. A setup bar lets you turn the camera and microphone on or off before you record.
- Counts down 3, 2, 1 (or 5, or off), and lets you pause and resume. Paused time is not in the recording.
- Records your microphone and your camera at the same time as the screen.
- Logs your cursor and clicks, so the editor can zoom in on clicks, glide the cursor and show click ripples.
- Opens a built-in editor with a 60 fps preview: edit zooms, trim, choose a background, and place a camera bubble.
- Saves your edits on their own while you work.
- Exports MP4 at 60 fps in 1080p or 4K, as H.264 or HEVC, in 16:9, 1:1 or 9:16. The export matches the preview.

## Requirements

- macOS 15 or later.
- A Mac that can run Metal.
- The download works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## Install

1. Download `notscreenstudio-0.1.0-macos.zip` and unzip it.
2. Move `NotScreenStudio.app` to Applications and open it. A circle icon appears in the menu bar.
3. This build is not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch. Try to open the app once and click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NotScreenStudio.app` in Terminal.
4. Open **Permissions…** from the menu bar icon and allow Screen Recording, Camera and Microphone. Relaunch the app once after allowing Screen Recording.

You can check the download against the `.sha256` file: `shasum -a 256 -c notscreenstudio-0.1.0-macos.zip.sha256`.

## Known limitations

- The app is not notarized yet, and it is signed ad hoc, so macOS shows a warning on first launch. A new version can ask for permissions again.
- Wallpapers that macOS lists only as downloads are not shown. Dynamic wallpapers show one still image.
- The recorder can capture system audio, but there is no switch for it yet. Recordings contain your microphone only.
- Export is MP4 only.
